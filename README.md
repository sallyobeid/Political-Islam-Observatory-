# Political-Islam-Observatory-
An open research initiative dedicated to documenting, analyzing, and mapping political Islamist movements, their ideologies, organizational structures, and transnational networks. The repository provides structured datasets, research methodologies, policy briefs, and open-source intelligence to support evidence-based research and public policy.
# Political Islam Observatory

An open research initiative dedicated to documenting,
analyzing,
and understanding political Islamist movements through evidence-based research,
structured data,
and open methodologies.

## Mission

To create the world's most comprehensive open research platform
for political Islam.

## Vision

Open Knowledge.
Transparent Methodology.
Evidence-Based Analysis.

## What We Build

- Open datasets
- Country profiles
- Research reports
- Policy briefs
- Interactive maps
- AI-assisted research tools

## Principles

- Evidence over opinion
- Transparency
- Reproducibility
- Open collaboration
